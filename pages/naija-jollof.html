Nigerian Jollof Rice

Is there a difference between Nigerian and Ghanaian jollof? Yes. Does the author know what it is? Absolutely not! But that doesn't matter, today you will learn how to cook jollof rice just like he does!

Ingredients

  1. Rice (preferably long-grained)
  2. Water
  3. Fresh Tomato + Fresh Pepper Paste (purchase separately and blend together, then boil)
  4. Tomato Paste (From the store, should be dark and thick, may contain pepper and onions)
  5. Onions
  6. Curry, Thyme, and Other Spices
  7. Bay Leaves
  8. Literally any type of cooking oil (usually veg. oil or palm oil)
  9. Salt
  10. Stock Cubes
  11. Stock
  12. Protein
  13. Vegetables (optional)
