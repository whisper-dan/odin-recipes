Nigerian Jollof Rice

Is there a difference between Nigerian and Ghanaian jollof? Yes. Does the author know what it is? Absolutely not! But that doesn't matter, today you will learn how to cook jollof rice just like he does!

Ingredients

  1. Rice (preferably long-grained)
  2. Water
  3. Fresh Tomato + Fresh Pepper Paste (purchase separately and blend together, then boil)
  4. Tomato Paste (From the store, should be dark and thick, may contain pepper and onions)
  5. Onions
  6. Curry, Thyme, and Other Spices
  7. Bay Leaves
  8. Literally any type of cooking oil (usually veg. oil or palm oil)
  9. Salt
  10. Stock Cubes
  11. Stock
  12. Protein
  13. Vegetables (optional)

Steps

  1. Get all ingredients ready (wash the rice, make sure the stock and tomato+pepper paste aren't frozen).
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry onions in it till it's golden brown.
  3. While frying onions, add all spices to the oil. This will make the food taste much better.
  4. Add tomato paste (store-bought) to the fried onions.
  5. Mix it around.
  6. Add your tomato+pepper paste to the pot, to the desired level of spicyness.
  7. Add stock to the pot and wait for it to heat up.
  8. Taste the stock. If it's not spicy or salty enough, add salt, tomato+pepper paste or stock cubes as appropriate, keeping in mind that the rice is unseasoned and will dilute whatever you can taste right now.
  9. Add rice
  10. Add just enough water to cover the rice, and then put some waterproof membrane unver the pot cover to prevent excessive moisture loss.
  11. Let it cook.
  12. Add protein.
  13. Fry some veggies in a pan (optional).
  14. Add vegetables (optional).
  15. Once the rice is soft and dry enough, turn off the fire.

Extra Info

  • If there is too much liquid in your rice, peel some potatoes and put them in there to soak up the excess moisture.
  • If there is not enough liquid in your rice, pour more water into it.
  • If the rice has soaked up the water but it's not soft enough, add water and turn down the heat so it softens up some more.
  • If you finish cooking and the rice doesn't have enough flavor, add salt. If it's still bad, don't fret. Mistakes are the price we pay for mastery.

That's it for the jollof rice recipe. Try it out. Enjoy.
