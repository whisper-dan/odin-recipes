Donuts

This is really a special recipe to the author, because who doesn't love donuts? They're not as popular as cake, but they're still a beloved pastry. Be warned, cooking this from start to end could take up to 5 hours, mostly because of wait times while the dough rises

Tools

  1. Cake Mixer (one that can mix dough)
  2. Large Flat Surface (for kneading and shaping donuts)

Ingredients

  1. Flour 4 cups
  2. Sugar 1/2 cup
  3. Instant Yeast 3 tsp
  4. Salt 1 tsp
  5. Powdered Milk 1 tbsp
  6. Cinnamon 1 tsp (optional)
  7. Milk 2 cups
  8. Butter 1/2 cup
  9. Vanilla 2 tsp
  10. Eggs 4

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl
  2. Mix wet ingredients in another bowl
  3. Make a hole in the dry igredients, pour wet ingredients in
  4. Mix dry and wet ingredients a little bit manually
  5. Put everything into the mixer
  6. When it's mixed together, stop the mixer and put a little oil on the surface where you will knead the dough. Put some oil on your hands too, because the dough will be a bit sticky
  7. Knead the dough until it stops being sticky. When it's done, it will maintain its shape more easily and if you stretch it thin and shine a light on it, you'll be able to see the light pass through it (window pane test). Getting to this point can take up to 30 minutes
  8. When the dough is done needing, put it in an oil-greased bowl and leave it in an oven with the light on for 60 to 90 mins while the yeast works on it.
  9. When the time is up, the dough should have doubled in size. Push it down with like 2 rounds of kneading, then roll the dough and press circles into it, or shape donuts by hand.
  10. Allow the donut shaped dough to rise again (60 to 90 mins).
  11. Fry the donuts on low heat (test with 1 donut to get the heat right, then fry the rest)
  12. Optional: Top the donuts with a glaze or simply coat them with sugar

This recipe takes a while, but it's 100% worth it.
